## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Kall helps you move prepared job applications across the finish line.

Kall's backend does the heavy lifting on the web -- tailoring a resume and
cover letter to a role, checking a submission for readiness issues. This app
covers the last mile: reviewing what Kall prepared and approving it from your
phone, wherever you are.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- See every application Kall is tracking for you, at a glance.
- Open an application to review its readiness issues before it goes out.
- Confirm each review item and approve the application when it's ready.
- Start your day with the Morning Brief: a quick read-only summary of what
  needs your attention.

Kall signs you in with the same account as the Kall web app, so your
applications, tailored documents, and progress stay in sync across both.

This app is scoped to the review-and-approve loop -- it's a companion to the
full Kall web app, not a replacement for it. Documents, opportunity search,
and your public career page are managed on the web for now.
```
(1050 characters)
