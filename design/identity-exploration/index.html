01

Edition

Editorial. Tactile. Personally meaningful.

RECOMMENDED

The premiseA career is an evolving body of work. Edition gives it the attention of a well-edited publication.

Product translationSerif display, direct labels, quiet rules, warm paper, and one vermilion action. Top navigation keeps the workspace open.

The tradeoffDisplay serifs need restraint on dense or small screens. Use the clear sans-serif for controls, evidence, and document details.

02

Vector

Structured. Focused. Technically confident.

The premiseTurn uncertainty into a sequence of legible decisions. Vector emphasizes precision without dashboards full of metrics.

Product translationCompact sidebar, strong sans-serif hierarchy, defined surfaces, monospace metadata, and a single pale chartreuse action.

The tradeoffIt can feel like a technical work tool. Keep career reflection and personal history warm in language and spacing.

03

Horizon

Open. Clear. Quietly optimistic.

The premiseCreate enough space to see a future clearly. Horizon makes a serious product feel easy to approach.

Product translationHumanist sans-serif, airy blue surfaces, rounded containers, cobalt actions, and a persistent desktop sidebar.

The tradeoffBlue is a crowded category. Its distinction needs to come from the sculptural K and the quality of the product language.

RECOMMENDATION

Move forward
with Edition.

Edition makes Kall feel like a personal career workspace rather than a recruiting funnel or an analytics console. It gives the product a warmer identity without borrowing the forest language of other studio products.

Use its editorial display selectively. Preserve a clear sans-serif for forms and evidence. Keep job fit explained in plain language and use the accent for a next action, not urgency.

What needs selection

  1. Choose Edition, Vector, or Horizon as the identity direction.
  2. Approve the proposed navigation and page hierarchy below, or request a revision.

What happens after selection

Refine the chosen mark into an original vector master, test 16px/icon/monochrome use, finalize font licensing and tokens, then apply the approved page migration incrementally. The current production brand remains unchanged until that decision.

Strategy, work history, record, achievements, references, and Growth. Growth develops future skills; it does not replace job-fit evidence.
