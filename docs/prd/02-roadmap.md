# Kall Product Roadmap

**Baseline:** 1.0 Alpha

## Alpha - Current Foundation

- Dark Nordic web foundation and shared navigation.
- Morning Brief using stored user data and deterministic readiness signals.
- Career Profiles with editable targeting, compensation, location, work-mode, travel, and relocation preferences.
- Resume Studio with library metadata, readiness checks, and per-profile defaults.
- Opportunities and job intelligence with strengths, gaps, and evidence.
- Applications Workspace with Preparing, Needs Review, Approved, Submitted, and Closed states.
- Candidate profile resources, field-level privacy, work authorization, and EEO controls.

## MVP Integration

- Unified first-run onboarding.
- Create Career Profile during onboarding.
- Upload and review a resume.
- Transition into the live Morning Brief.
- Connect Brief recommendations to Opportunity Review and Applications.
- Consolidate navigation and responsive app shells.
- Add analytics, accessibility validation, contract tests, and end-to-end coverage.

## Private Beta

- Invite-only user cohort.
- Morning Brief usefulness testing.
- Career Profile and Resume Studio usability testing.
- Opportunity relevance and explanation evaluation.
- Application review safety validation.
- Performance, reliability, and onboarding improvements.

## Post-MVP

- Interview records, preparation workspace, and scheduling.
- Offer, negotiation, accepted, and declined states.
- Career Timeline and richer Career Health dimensions.
- Learning plans, certifications, and renewal reminders.
- Network, mentors, references, and relationship management.
- Public career portfolio and optional recruiter discovery.
- Recruiter, hiring-manager, university, and enterprise experiences.

## Roadmap Rule

New ideas must be classified as Alpha correction, MVP integration, beta learning, or post-MVP expansion. Features may not be represented as implemented until backed by working product behavior and stored domain data.
