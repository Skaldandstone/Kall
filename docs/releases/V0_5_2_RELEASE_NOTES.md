# Kall v0.5.2

Kall v0.5.2 connects structured resume and job parsing to deterministic, auditable match intelligence.

## Included

- Versioned resume-to-job scoring
- Requirement coverage and evidence records
- Verified achievement matching
- Recommended resume selection
- Persistent user overrides
- Job Intelligence review workspace
- Ownership and sensitive-data boundaries

## Scoring principles

The default-resume preference is capped at five points. Unsupported requirements remain visible gaps. Only verified achievements may contribute evidence. Rejected achievements are excluded. Scores are deterministic and store their scoring version for future audit and recalculation.

## Next

Kall v0.5.3 will use the selected resume and verified evidence to propose truthful, side-by-side tailoring changes that require user review before generation or application use.
